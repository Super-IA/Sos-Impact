1. Controller

The controller is Super-ia, developer of SOS Impact. Contact: amazfit.sosimpact@gmail.com.

2. What the app does

SOS Impact is an app for Zepp OS watches (Amazfit with Zepp OS 3.0 or later) that detects strong impacts followed by stillness and, if you do not respond, alerts the Telegram contacts you configured.

4. No server of our own

SOS Impact has no server of its own. The developer does not receive, see or store your data, and uses no analytics, advertising or trackers. Data travels from your watch to your phone (over Bluetooth, through the Zepp app) and from your phone to Telegram.

The only exception is payment. The trial and licence are handled by KiezelPay, a third-party service. Your phone sends it a randomly generated anonymous identifier, your watch model and the install date (and, as with any internet connection, your IP address). It never receives your alerts, location or medical data. Payment is made at www.kzl.io and is governed by KiezelPay's privacy policy.

5. Third parties

  • Telegram (Telegram Messenger Inc. / Telegram FZ-LLC) receives and delivers the alerts to your contacts and processes them under its own privacy policy: telegram.org/privacy.
  • CallMeBot (only if you enable the Telegram call or the WhatsApp alert): external service that makes the voice call or forwards the message by WhatsApp. It receives your name, a fixed sentence or the alert text with the link to your location, and the username or phone of your contacts. Its policy: callmebot.com.
  • KiezelPay (kiezelpay.com): handles the free trial and the one-time payment. It receives an anonymous identifier, the watch model and the install date, and processes the payment under its own privacy policy.
  • Zepp Health / Amazfit: the app runs inside the watch and the Zepp app, which process data under their own policy.
  • Your contacts receive your location, name and medical data. Inform the people you add.

6. Legal basis

Processing is based on your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and, for any health information you choose to include, Art. 9(2)(a) of the GDPR), which you give by ticking the acceptance box in the settings. You can withdraw it at any time by unticking it, whereupon the app stops monitoring and sending alerts, or by uninstalling the app.

7. Retention and deletion

Data remains on your watch and your phone until you delete it or uninstall the app. Messages sent to Telegram are retained according to Telegram; you can delete them from its app.

8. Your rights

You may exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability by writing to amazfit.sosimpact@gmail.com. Since the developer does not store your data, you control most of it directly on your watch, your phone and Telegram. You may lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country (in Spain, the AEPD).

9. Security

Your bot token is stored in your phone settings: do not share it. Communications with Telegram use HTTPS. No system is infallible.

10. Minors

The app is not intended for children under 14 without the supervision of their mother, father or guardian.

11. Use notice

SOS Impact is not a medical device or a certified safety device. It does not guarantee that it will detect every fall or that alerts will arrive (they depend on the watch, battery, phone, coverage, GPS and Telegram). It does not call emergency services: in an emergency, call 112 (the emergency number in Spain and the EU; use your local emergency number elsewhere).

12. Changes

If this policy changes, it will be updated here with a new date and, if the change is important, your acceptance will be requested again.
