SOS Impact watches over you through your watch and detects a strong blow followed by stillness. If that happens, it vibrates, sounds an alarm and gives you a few seconds to press I'M OK. If you do not respond, it sends an alert via Telegram to your contacts with your location on the map, your heart rate and any details you choose to include (name, allergies, medication).

What it does

  • Two-phase detection: impact, then stillness. If you move normally, it does not alert.
  • Countdown with vibration and an audible alarm, which can be cancelled with one button.
  • Alerts several Telegram contacts, with an "I've seen it" button and reminders if nobody responds.
  • Live location for the minutes following the alert.
  • Manual SOS: tap the screen three times.
  • Adjustable sensitivity and profiles (Normal, Senior, Athlete).
  • The alert includes heart rate and blood oxygen when the watch has measured them.
  • Optional, experimental extra: alert also via WhatsApp.
  • Low battery warning and an optional daily "Are you OK?" check.
  • Fall simulation to test without risk. Available in Spanish, English, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese and Dutch.
  • No server of our own: your data goes from your watch to your phone and to Telegram.

Compatible watches

Current Amazfit watches with Zepp OS 3.0 or later: Balance, GTR 4, GTS 4, Active, Bip 5 Unity, Bip 6, Cheetah, Falcon, T-Rex 3, T-Rex Ultra and their variants. The app must be open on the watch, with the screen on, to read the motion sensors.

7-day free trial. After that, the app is unlocked with a one-time payment of €4.99 through KiezelPay (the watch shows a code that you enter at www.kzl.io/code).

Important notice

SOS Impact is not a medical device or a certified safety device and does not guarantee that it will detect every fall or that the alert will arrive. It does not call emergency services: in an emergency, call 112 (the emergency number in Spain and the EU; use your local emergency number elsewhere).
