Getting started

  1. Create your Telegram bot. In Telegram open @BotFather, type /newbot and follow the steps. It will give you a token in this form: 123456789:AAH….
  2. Message the bot. Open your bot and send /start. Every person who should receive the alerts has to do the same with your bot.
  3. Configure the app. In the Zepp app on your phone, open the SOS Impact settings: tick the privacy policy box, paste the token and tap Detect chat ID. Then tap Send test alert.
  4. Enter your name and, if you wish, your medical information (allergies, medication…). It will be included in the alert.
  5. Open the app on the watch. It should say "WATCHING". Leave the app open and the screen on.

Unlocking the app (payment)

  1. After the 7-day trial the app locks and the watch shows a BUY button.
  2. Tap it (the phone needs the Zepp app open and Bluetooth on): a payment code appears on the watch.
  3. Enter that code at www.kzl.io/code and complete the one-time €2.99 + taxes payment with KiezelPay. The watch unlocks by itself within a few seconds.
  4. Already paid and the watch asks you to pay again (for example after a reset)? Go to www.kzl.io/unlock.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work with the screen off or in the background?

No. Zepp OS closes apps a few seconds after the screen turns off and does not allow reading the accelerometer in the background. That is why SOS Impact keeps the screen on while watching (the "Always on" option, which is the recommended one), which uses more battery. If you choose to let the screen turn off, the app stops detecting while it is off and reopens when the watch wakes up.

Does it call 112 or send SMS?

No. Zepp OS does not allow apps to make calls or send SMS. The alert is sent via Telegram and includes a reminder to call 112 (the emergency number in Spain and the EU; use your local number elsewhere). Optionally, you can enable a voice call via Telegram to your contacts (in the settings, "Telegram call"): each contact must send /start to @CallMeBot_txtbot once. The number shown in the alert defaults to 112 (for English, a generic reminder to call your local emergency number); you can set your own country's number in the settings ("Emergency number").

What health data does the alert carry?

If the watch has recent readings (from the last 6 hours), the alert includes heart rate and blood oxygen (SpO₂). This is indicative only, not a medical device. If your watch does not measure one of them, that line does not appear.

What is the low battery warning?

When the watch battery drops below 15%, SOS Impact alerts your contacts once via Telegram, because falls are not detected while the watch is off. It re-arms when you recharge above 30%. You can turn it off in the settings.

The alert arrives without a location

The watch's GPS needs open sky or to be next to a window to get a position. Check that the watch's location is turned on and that the app has permission. If there is no position, the alert is still sent with "location not available".

It triggers by accident / it does not trigger

Change the sensitivity in the settings (Low, Medium, High) or the profile. The settings include a Diagnostics section with the latest events; send it to us so we can fine-tune.

I changed phones

The licence stays on the watch. The Telegram settings are saved on the phone and you may have to enter them again.

How do I test the app without throwing myself on the floor?

With SOS Impact open on the watch (watching), go to the phone settings and tap Simulate a fall. The watch shows the countdown with vibration and sound, but does not send any alert. To test real sending, use Send test alert.

What is the daily check?

Optional. In the settings you choose an hour (1-22). That day, from that hour onwards, the watch asks "Are you OK?" and you have 60 seconds to press "I'm OK". If you do not respond, your contacts are alerted. It asks only once a day and only if the app is watching with the screen on.

Which languages is it available in?

Spanish, English, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese and Dutch. You change it in the phone settings (Language section).

The Telegram call does not ring or is slow

It uses the free CallMeBot service, which sometimes queues calls or is overloaded. Enter each contact's Telegram @username (a phone number often fails), have each contact send /start to @CallMeBot_txtbot, and in Telegram → Privacy → Calls allow calls from "Everybody". It is an optional extra: the Telegram message alert is the main one. Experimental feature: Telegram may block these calls as spam and CallMeBot no longer answers direct messages for free (it charges Stars), so it may not ring. Do not pay to fix it: the Telegram message alert is the reliable one.

Can I receive the alert via WhatsApp?

Yes, as an experimental and optional extra (settings → WhatsApp). It uses the free, unofficial CallMeBot service, which may be slow or fail, and it has no "I've seen it" button or reminders. Each contact must enable it once by saving the number indicated on callmebot.com and sending it "I allow callmebot to send me messages" via WhatsApp; they will receive a numeric key and you enter it as +34600111222:key. The message carries your name and the link to your location, without medical data. The Telegram alert remains the main one.
