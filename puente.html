Important. This is an extra for Android: it is not an emergency service and does not replace 112/911. It depends on internet, battery and Android not closing the app. Always test it before relying on it.

What it is

SOS Impact Bridge is a free Android app that replaces ntfy + MacroDroid. You install one app, type the ntfy topic and the phone number that should get the alert, and that is it: when the watch detects a fall or you press SOS, your phone sends an SMS with the alert phrase and a link to your location, automatically and only once per alert.

You need: an Android phone with the Zepp and SOS Impact apps set up and a SIM card with SMS. You do not need to install ntfy or MacroDroid. On iPhone the SMS cannot be automated.

Step-by-step setup

  1. Enable ntfy in SOS Impact. In the Zepp app open the SOS Impact settings, find the ntfy alert box and tap Enable ntfy. A secret topic (sosimpact-…) appears. Copy it and do not publish it.
  2. Download and install the app. Download SOS-Impact-Puente.apk on your phone. If it does not download (for example, if you opened this guide from the Zepp app), copy this address and open it in Chrome: https://super-ia.github.io/Sos-Impact/SOS-Impact-Puente.apk. Android will ask for permission to install apps from unknown sources (this is normal: the app is not on Google Play because Play does not allow an app to send SMS). Accept and install. On Android 13 or later, before granting the SMS permission (step 4) go to Settings → Apps → SOS Impact Bridge → ⋮ → Allow restricted settings.
  3. Type the topic and the phone number. Open SOS Impact Bridge, paste the topic (or the full link) and type the phone number that will get the SMS, with country code (for example +34600111222). You do not need the ntfy app or any ntfy.sh link: the Bridge reads the alerts directly. You can add up to two numbers. Nothing else needs changing.
  4. Calls (optional). Tick “Also call the first phone number (after the SMS)” if you want the phone to call the first number after the SMS. Android will ask for the phone permission and, to be able to call with the phone locked or another app open, the Display over other apps permission: accept both. Try the Test call button first.
  5. Tap “Start watching”. Android will ask for permission to send SMS and for notifications: accept both. Then it will ask you to remove the battery restriction: accept it too, it keeps the app awake.
  6. Manufacturer settings (Xiaomi, Huawei, Oppo…). Tap Permissions and battery and allow the app to auto-start. On Xiaomi, also go to Settings → Apps → Permissions → Other permissions and set “Send SMS messages” to Allow, and pin the app with the padlock in the recent apps screen so the cleaner does not close it. Important: in Settings → Apps → SOS Impact Bridge turn off “Pause app activity if not used” (when on, the phone removes the SMS permission and notifications) and set battery to No restrictions.
  7. Test it. Tap Send test SMS: an SMS must arrive on the phone. Then, in the SOS Impact settings (Zepp app) tap Send test via ntfy: another SMS with the test text must arrive. Finally do a manual SOS on the watch: a single SMS arrives with the alert phrase and the map link. The app status will show “SMS sent to 1 phone(s)”.

How it works

The watch alerts the Zepp app on your phone, which publishes the alert to ntfy. SOS Impact Bridge keeps a connection to your topic and, when the alert arrives, sends the SMS through the SIM card set as “default for SMS” in Android. An SMS is sent only for the first alert (and for the ntfy test): not for the follow-up messages, it is not repeated if ntfy resends the alert, and nothing older than 30 minutes is sent.

If it does not work

SymptomWhat to check
The status says “No connection, retrying…”Check that the phone has data or Wi-Fi and that the topic is typed correctly.
“SMS permission is missing” or the SMS permission is greyed outAndroid settings → Apps → SOS Impact Bridge → tap ⋮ (top right) → Allow restricted settings (confirm with your fingerprint or PIN) → go back to Permissions → SMS → Allow. Android 13 and later block SMS for apps installed from outside Google Play until you do this.
The test SMS does not arriveCheck that the SIM set as “default for SMS” has coverage and credit/SMS, and that the phone number is correct, with country code.
It works for a while and then stopsThe system closes the app: remove battery restrictions, enable auto-start and pin the app (step 5).
It does not call, but the SMS is sentCheck the phone and Display over other apps permissions (Settings → Apps → SOS Impact Bridge) and try Test call. On Xiaomi also allow “Display pop-up windows while running in the background”.
I want to stop using itTap Stop watching. In SOS Impact you can Disable ntfy.

Privacy

The app only connects to ntfy (ntfy.sh or your server) to read the alerts of your topic and uses the SMS permission only to send the alert SMS to the numbers you type. It has no accounts, ads or analytics, and it does not send your data anywhere else. The secret topic is the only “password” of the alert: do not share it.
